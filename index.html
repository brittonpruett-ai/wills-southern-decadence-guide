The St. Ann orbit

Oz, Bourbon Pub & Parade, Good Friends, Café Lafitte in Exile, Napoleon’s Itch and Golden Lantern provide different versions of the Quarter’s queer nightlife.

Leather and bear current

The Phoenix and Rawhide traditionally pull a more leather-oriented crowd. Check each venue’s current event listing before going.

Femme-forward

Her Haus centers queer women, trans and nonbinary guests while welcoming the broader community. Detailed 2026 programming is forthcoming.

Performance-forward

Look for drag, cabaret, burlesque and alternative queer programming at venues such as AllWays Lounge and Bourbon Pub. Verify the weekend calendar before departure.

Legal grown-up entertainment

Choose licensed strip clubs, go-go shows, burlesque, leather nightlife or ticketed adult-themed events. Pay performers, respect boundaries and remember that enthusiasm is not consent.

The unavailable button

“Summon a hooker” is not a feature. This guide can find dinner, dancing and legal adult entertainment. It cannot order a person like late-night room service. Will will survive this administrative setback.

When he wants polish

Build around a reservation, a thoughtful wine list and one excellent bar seat. Confirm holiday-weekend hours and book early.

When he needs food immediately

Prioritize late-night po-boys, burgers, breakfast or bar food near the current location. Eat before the evening becomes a documentary.

Will, Be Serious: after-hours operating standards

  • Meet new people in public first and tell someone where you are going.
  • Verify the rideshare vehicle and driver in the app before entering.
  • Keep identification, one payment card and emergency cash separate.
  • Consent must be clear, mutual and ongoing. Intoxication complicates consent.
  • Do not accept unidentified substances or leave drinks unattended.
  • Carry water, charge the phone and use the bathroom before declaring the night “just getting started.”
  • If something feels wrong, leave. Southern manners are not a hostage agreement.
